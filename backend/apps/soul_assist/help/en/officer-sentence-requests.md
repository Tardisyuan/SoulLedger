Sentence requests ask to change a soul's sentence plan: amend it (add or remove a station) or reopen the judgment. Statuses: pending (awaiting the original judge's decision), accepted, rejected, withdrawn.
- Approve / Reject: the plan's home hall (or an administrator); needs the judgment execute permission. Accepting applies at once; accepting a reopen opens a retrial at once. A rejection may carry a reason.
- Withdraw: the hall that filed the request (or an administrator).
- Cancelling a whole sentence plan is separate: home hall only, with a reason, and it needs the sentence plan cancel permission.
The page shows one row per plan with a pending request; rows waiting for this officer are marked.
Disposition (sidebar: Reincarnation Disposition) has three sections: to execute, executing, expired. Execute needs the disposition execute permission and is refused if the soul is in the wrong state; Arrange rebirth opens the soul's page.
