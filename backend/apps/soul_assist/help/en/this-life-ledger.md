The "This life" page is your record for this life: name and soul code (it can be copied), civilization, state, birth, death and origin; while residing, the header reads "Residing in X · belongs to Y".
"This life's ledger" is the journal of merits and demerits: each entry is one merit or one demerit, and opening it shows the detail; "Balance, this life" is what merits and demerits come to once set against each other. Entries are recorded by the hall; you cannot add or remove them yourself. Categories and wording follow your home civilization's rules.
The page also holds this life's judgments (court, presiding judge, verdict, destination and term), dispositions (carried out or not) and rebirth applications; when there is no case, no disposition or no application yet, the section says so.
The sentence plan is in the "My sentence" section, see sentence-plan; the state tag is explained in soul-states; earlier lives are on the "Past lives" page.
