Settings > Account security > "Devices and sign-ins" has two parts.
Devices: the devices signed in to this account, with when each was last seen; "This phone" is the one in your hand. Other devices have a "Sign out" button; after you tap it that device must sign in again, and no password is needed. This phone has no such button; use "Sign out" at the bottom of Settings.
Sign-ins: recent sign-ins, failed ones included; the page says how long they are kept. Identical failures in a row are merged into one line saying how many times and why: wrong password, wrong code, or face not recognised. A sign-in with a recovery code is marked as such.
If you see a device or record you do not recognise: sign that device out first, then change your password. Changing the password signs out everything except this phone.
