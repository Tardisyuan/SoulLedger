Capture applies only in the Chinese underworld: when a living soul reaches its fated date, the city god issues a capture warrant, the escorts capture the soul, and handing it over registers the death.
- Sidebar "Awaiting a capture warrant": this hall's underworld souls whose fated date has come, still alive and with no open warrant, earliest fated date first; you can "Issue" straight from the list. Nothing issues warrants automatically; an officer has to.
- The "Capture" block on the soul's detail page: issue the warrant, then mark it captured, then hand over to the city god; until hand-over it can be revoked, with a required reason. Viewing needs the view capture permission; issuing and revoking need the issue capture warrant permission; capturing and handing over need the carry out capture permission.
- Escort: if you do not choose, it follows the soul's karma: more demerit than merit sends Ox Head and Horse Face, otherwise Black and White Impermanence. The issuing city god is matched by the soul's place of origin, by the same rule as city god case intake.
- Hand-over is the death registration: the date of death is the day of capture, the case goes to the issuing city god for review, and the soul arrives at the Earth God's Temple of the thirteen stations. If the soul is no longer alive, hand-over is refused and nothing is changed.
- If the death was registered another way first (death sync, an earth god, declaring death by hand), the warrant is issued with the death and marked handed over: an open warrant is handed over, and an underworld soul with none gets one. The soul sees this handed-over warrant in its own app.
- Why issuing is refused: the soul is not of the Chinese underworld; it is no longer alive; its fated date has not come or is not recorded; or it already has an open warrant.
