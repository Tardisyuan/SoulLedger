The rebirth quota limits how many rebirths a hall may do each month for each path. Open "Tenant Management" (administrators only), tap "Edit settings" on the hall's row, and the dialog has a "Rebirth quota" block. Egyptian halls do not have this block.
There is one row per path, plus an "Unclassified" row. Two columns: "Monthly limit" is what you enter, and "Done this month" is how many have been done this month; the count shows only in your own hall.
Blank means no limit; zero means none this month for that path; anything else must be zero or a larger whole number, within a maximum, and a bad entry is flagged. If you set it below what is already done this month, the row says that path takes no more this month.
When a path is full, souls awaiting rebirth on it wait their turn until next month or until the limit is raised; nothing is lost. The month's rebirth quota of each hall is also on the halls overview.
The same dialog also holds the rebirth application cooldown, the roles required to use two-step verification, the hall's visiting address, and the switch that lets souls change their own contact email; they all take effect together when you save.
