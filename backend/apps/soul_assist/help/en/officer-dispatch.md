Dispatch moves a soul to reside in another civilization's hall for a time.
Flow: draft, then proposed (awaiting approval), then approved or rejected (or cancelled); an approved dispatch is executed, the soul is in residence, and it ends with the return home.
- Propose ("Propose Dispatch", or "Transfer…" from the souls list): needs the dispatch manage permission. Only the officer who started a draft may edit or submit it; the proposal needs a soul, a target civilization and a written reason of at least twenty characters; it is refused while the soul's sentence plan is in progress.
- Approve and execute: the target hall only (the dispatch approve and execute permissions).
- Reject: the target hall only as well (the dispatch reject permission). An administrator who belongs to no hall may also approve, reject and execute. Once a proposal is decided, the proposer can no longer edit its reason.
- End residence: the soul's home hall or an administrator (the dispatch return permission), with a reason. The soul also returns home by itself once its disposition, or its stay at that station of a sentence plan, has been carried out. Ending a residence by hand is refused while the soul still has an open judgment.
The "Pending Proposals" tab is the target hall's queue; "History" lists every dispatch record where your hall is the source or the target, pending ones included; an administrator who belongs to no hall sees every hall's. The other hall's officers show as the hall name only, not who they are. Notices of proposals, decisions and cancellations go only to officers holding the dispatch read permission.
Cross-realm judgments are a separate page: the initiating hall adds participants and then uses "Activate judgment"; after activation no more participants can be added. Writes need the cross-realm judgment create permission.
