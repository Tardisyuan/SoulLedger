Dispatch moves a soul to reside in another civilization's hall for a time.
Flow: draft, then proposed (awaiting approval), then approved or rejected (or cancelled); an approved dispatch is executed, the soul is in residence, and it ends with the return home.
- Propose (Start dispatch, or Transfer from the souls list): needs dispatch manage. Only the officer who started a draft may edit or submit it; the proposal needs a soul, a target civilization and a written reason; it is refused while the soul's sentence plan is in progress.
- Approve and Execute: the target hall only (dispatch approve / execute).
- Reject: either hall involved (dispatch reject).
- End residence: the soul's home hall (dispatch return), with a reason.
The Pending Proposals tab is the target hall's queue; History lists the rest.
Cross-realm judgments are a separate page: the initiating hall adds participants and then Activates the joint judgment; after activation no more participants can be added. Writes need the cross-realm judgment create permission.
