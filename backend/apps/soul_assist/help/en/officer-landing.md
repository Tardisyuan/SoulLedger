The site's landing page (the root address) can be viewed without signing in: an introduction to "SoulLedger · Cross-civilization soul management system" at the top, then one line for each of the four civilizations (the Chinese Diyu, European Heaven and Hell, the Egyptian Duat and the Greek Underworld), plus the language and light-or-dark theme switches.
The "Console" link at the top right opens the dashboard when you are signed in and the sign-in page when you are not; when signed in, the page also names the signed-in account. The landing page shows no data and offers no actions.
