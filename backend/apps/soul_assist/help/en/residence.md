Residing means you were transferred to another civilization to serve or stay there; it does not change which civilization you belong to. The header shows "Residing in X, home Y".
While residing: rebirth and appeals are still handled by your home civilization; the circle shows the civilization you are residing in; after the station there is served you return home.
The register still uses your home civilization's wording.
