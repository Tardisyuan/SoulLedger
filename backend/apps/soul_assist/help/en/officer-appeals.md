This is about reviewing a soul's appeal against a concluded judgment, not an appeal of a rebirth application, which is in officer-rebirth-applications. After a soul files an appeal in the app, the case appears on the "Appeal reviews" page under "Judgment Queue" (judgment read permission needed).
Four tabs: Awaiting review, Under review, Closed (verdict stands, verdict changed, void) and Withdrawn. They are ordered by when the appeal was filed; columns show the case number, round, filed time, and time left to the deadline (for reference only, since the appeal is already filed); the reviewer shows only under "Under review". Open a row for the details.
Claiming: tap "Claim for review" (judgment execute permission). If you gave the original judgment, meaning you concluded or claimed it, the button is greyed with the reason: a different officer must review. One already claimed by someone else says so.
Deciding: after claiming, tap "Decide", and choose one of two. "Uphold the verdict"; or "Change the verdict", picking the new verdict, which cannot be the same as the original. The reviewer's note is required and the soul sees it; once sent, the soul is notified.
What a changed verdict does: stations of the sentence plan not yet served follow the new verdict; each station already served earns the soul one "compensation" merit entry, added to its merits and demerits. If the plan holds foreign stops brought in by a joint judgment, the dialog asks whether to keep all of them or remove all. A change is refused while the soul is on its way to the next station, or abroad and that stop is not served, or while the plan is being re-judged, suspended for good, or has a request waiting for the original judge; decide after that stop is served or the situation passes.
Escalation: after a review upholds the verdict, the soul may escalate. The appeal is then not reviewed by you alone but goes through your civilization's appeal court station by station: a rejection at any station leaves the verdict standing and you need not decide; when every station is cleared, the officer at the last station becomes the reviewer and decides here. While the court is sitting the appeal cannot be claimed or decided, and the soul cannot withdraw it.
How many appeals, the period and the statuses are the soul-side rules, and the soul may withdraw its own appeal; an appeal still open when the soul is devoured by Ammit is voided on the spot, with no change and no compensation. When an officer is handed over before deactivation, appeals in their review are handed over too.
