The offence applies only to Chinese underworld cases. The ten courts are divided by the kind of wrong they try, not by how heavy it is: the searchable "Offence" field at the top of the "Placement" section of the judgment desk groups offences by court (court, king, hell), and once chosen it says "If found guilty, sent to" that court.
- On a Failed verdict the court is chosen by the offence: the court that tries that kind of wrong, whatever the merit and demerit totals. Only courts two to nine can be chosen; the first and tenth courts do not punish and have no offences; the five heinous crimes go to the ninth court.
- With no offence recorded, a guilty soul's court is chosen by severity, as it was before offences existed; older cases go that way too.
- A case of the five heinous crimes cannot be found Passed: concluding, amending on appeal and approval all refuse it, and the desk greys "Passed" with the reason.
- Who can change it: anyone with the execute judgment permission, while the case is open; a case someone else has claimed, only the claimant. Once concluded the offence cannot be changed, because it has already decided the court. Recording an offence on another civilization's case is refused.
