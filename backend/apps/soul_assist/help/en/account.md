Sign in with your soul code and password; the code and the initial password are sent to you together by the hall office. On your first sign-in you must replace the initial password with one of your own; the initial password has an expiry, the page shows how long is left, and once expired an officer must reset it.
Forgot password: tap "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. If an email is bound to your soul account, enter it, receive a code, and set a new password; the code expires, and after too many wrong tries you must send a new one. After a successful reset every other device is signed out.
When several souls share one email address, only the first one to bind it receives the code; the others follow the no-email way below.
A soul with no bound email cannot reset its own password in the app: write to your hall office with your name and soul code; once the hall has confirmed who you are, it resets the password and gives you a temporary one. Signed out, you cannot write letters in the app, so ask a soul of your hall to pass it on, or explain in person at the hall's registry.
A new password cannot be too short, all digits, or a common one. "Sign out" is at the bottom of the "Settings" page.
Signing in by scanning: next to the soul code on the sign-in page there is "Scan"; point it at the QR code on the right of your delivery slip and the code fills in by itself, while you still type the password. A soul code uses only letters and digits, never 0, O, 1, I or L. Without camera access, turn the camera on for Soul Ledger in system settings, or tap "Type the number instead"; the camera only reads the code and nothing is photographed or saved.
Contact email: the "Contact email" row in Settings shows "Add" if there is none and "Change" if there is one; the forgotten-password code is sent there. It takes three steps: your current password first (plus the six digits from your authenticator if two-step verification is on), then the new address, then the code sent to the new address; once it is right the change is done, and the old address gets a "contact email changed" notice. After the password is verified you have a limited time to finish, otherwise start again. An address already used by another account is refused. Some halls do not allow changing it yourself; the page then says "To change your contact email, come to your hall with your delivery slip", and you go to the hall.
