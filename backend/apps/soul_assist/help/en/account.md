Sign in with your soul code and password; the code and the initial password are sent to you together by the hall office. On your first sign-in you must replace the initial password with one of your own; the initial password has an expiry, the page shows how long is left, and once expired an officer must reset it.
Forgot password: tap "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. If an email is bound to your soul account, enter it, receive a code, and set a new password; the code expires, and after too many wrong tries you must send a new one. After a successful reset every other device is signed out.
A soul with no bound email cannot reset its own password in the app: write to your hall office with your name and soul code; once the hall has confirmed who you are, it resets the password and gives you a temporary one. Signed out, you cannot write letters in the app, so ask a soul of your hall to pass it on, or explain in person at the hall's registry.
A new password cannot be too short, all digits, or a common one. "Sign out" is at the bottom of the "Settings" page.
