The "About & credits" page lists the third-party assets and services the app uses, with their licences and sources. It is read-only and changes no data; it opens from the user menu in the top bar after signing in, and cannot be viewed signed out.
It has five sections:
- Typefaces: the interface fonts and the Chinese fallback fonts, plus the lettering and seal fonts the plaque and seal load on demand, each with its licence.
- Open-source software: the open-source dependencies of the server, the web console, the app, and those shared by both.
- Texts and sources: the texts and references for the four civilizations; some are marked "Cited for comparison".
- Models and services: the models and services used by the assistant, the embeddings and the like; all configured per deployment, with the default one marked.
- Design and collaboration.
To replace or add an asset, ask the hall's administrator: the assets and the credits list live in the code, not in the console.
