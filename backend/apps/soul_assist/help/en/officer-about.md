The "About & credits" page lists the third-party typefaces and images the app uses, with their licences. It is read-only, opens from the user menu, can be viewed without signing in, and changes no data.
- Typefaces: the three interface font stacks and the Chinese fallback fonts, plus the lettering and seal fonts that the plaque and seal load on demand; all are used under the SIL Open Font License (OFL).
- Seal edges: the worn edges around each seal are masks made from museum scans, from the Museo Egizio in Turin and The Metropolitan Museum of Art. All were released under the Creative Commons public-domain dedication, so no attribution is legally required; the page still names each museum, object and source link.
To replace or add an asset, ask the hall's administrator: the assets and the credits list live in the code, not in the console.
