The "About & credits" page lists the third-party typefaces and ornament the app uses, with their licences. It is read-only, opens from the user menu, can be viewed without signing in, and changes no data.
- Typefaces: the three interface font stacks and the Chinese fallback fonts, plus the lettering and seal fonts that the plaque and seal load on demand; all are used under the SIL Open Font License (OFL).
- Ornament: the plaque and section ornament comes from Owen Jones's "The Grammar of Ornament", which is in the public domain.
To replace or add an asset, ask the hall's administrator: the assets and the credits list live in the code, not in the console.
