City god review applies only in the Chinese underworld: after a death is registered, the case first goes to the city god covering the soul's place of origin, and reaches the ten courts' queue only once that city god sends it on. The last and most specific place name in the origin decides which city god; if none matches, the hall's fallback city god takes it; with no fallback the case goes straight to the ten courts.
- Sidebar "City god review": cases awaiting review and cases returned for missing information. Officers under that city god can send a case on (note optional) or return it (note required); hall admins can also transfer it to another city god of the hall. Other officers can only read.
- While a case is with the city god, the ten courts cannot claim, reassign, defer or conclude it. An untimely death stays held in the City of Wrongful Death until its fated date even after it is sent on.
- "Settings › City gods": hall admins create city gods with the place names they cover and their officers, and may mark one as the fallback. Within a hall a place name belongs to one city god only; a city god with cases not yet sent on cannot be deleted.
- City god notifications (received, returned) can be switched off in your profile's notification settings.
