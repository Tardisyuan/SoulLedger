In Settings > Notifications you can turn each kind on or off: rebirth application results, judgment results, residence and return, and letters.
Notifications only say there is a result: a rebirth result says approved or rejected without the reason text; a judgment result carries no evidence or merit details; residence does not say where you are sent.
The lock screen never shows a rejection reason, judgment evidence, merit scores or a password. If notifications are off at the system level, your choices here are still saved but nothing is delivered until you allow them in the system.
