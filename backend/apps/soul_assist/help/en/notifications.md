In "Settings > Notifications" you can turn each kind on or off: rebirth application results, judgment results, residence and return, and letters; the "Social" group adds comments and replies and review results (your post or comment approved, hidden, deleted or restored). "Hall warnings" is always on and cannot be turned off. The "Notification history" entry on the same page lists every notification sent to you, delivered or not.
Notifications mostly only say there is a result: a rebirth result says approved or rejected without the reason text; a judgment result carries no evidence or merit details; residence does not say where you are sent.
One exception: when a report about your post, comment or account is resolved as a warning, the push includes the reason text the hall office wrote, and it shows on the lock screen. That push has no switch in Settings and cannot be turned off.
Apart from those warnings, and the reason an officer writes when hiding or deleting your post or comment, the lock screen never shows a rejection reason, judgment evidence, merit scores or a password. If notifications are off at the system level, your choices here are still saved but nothing is delivered until you allow them in the system.
