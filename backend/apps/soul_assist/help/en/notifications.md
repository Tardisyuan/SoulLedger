In "Settings > Notifications" you can turn each kind on or off: rebirth application results, judgment results, residence and return, and letters.
Notifications mostly only say there is a result: a rebirth result says approved or rejected without the reason text; a judgment result carries no evidence or merit details; residence does not say where you are sent.
One exception: when a report about your post, comment or account is resolved as a warning, the push includes the reason text the hall office wrote, and it shows on the lock screen. That push has no switch in Settings and cannot be turned off.
Apart from those warnings, the lock screen never shows a rejection reason, judgment evidence, merit scores or a password. If notifications are off at the system level, your choices here are still saved but nothing is delivered until you allow them in the system.
Notification history: "Settings > Notification history" lists every notification sent to you, newest first, including ones you had turned off and ones that were not delivered; undelivered ones carry a "Not delivered" tag. It loads a page at a time: tap "More" for the next page, or pull down to refresh. Tapping an item that has a destination goes where the notification would have gone; if the destination no longer exists the item is greyed out and opens nothing.
