Choose it in Settings > Language. It takes effect at once without signing in again, and is saved to your account; after you sign in on another device, the App applies your account's language.
It changes the interface and prompts; file content (verdicts, statements, reasons) stays in its original language and is not translated.
Ask answers in Chinese when the interface is Chinese, and in English otherwise.
