"Dedication of merit" is one kind of merit entry in this life's entries: a rite your living relatives held for you, entered by an officer. It counts as merit only, never as demerit.
Only souls being judged or serving a sentence can receive it; souls in other states, such as living or already reborn, cannot. You cannot add or change one yourself; ask your living relatives to contact an officer of the hall to have it entered.
Once entered it counts toward this life's balance like any other merit, and appears on the "This life" page under "Dedication of merit".
