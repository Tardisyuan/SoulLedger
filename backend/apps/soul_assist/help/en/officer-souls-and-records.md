Souls (sidebar: Soul Management): Create soul needs soul create; Export, Move to recycle bin (soul delete) and Transfer (dispatch manage, one soul at a time) are in the batch bar. On a soul's page the actions follow its state: an alive soul can be marked dead and sent to judgment (soul die), a soul under judgment gets Start judgment (judgment create), a disposed soul can be reborn (reincarnation reborn); Edit needs soul update.
Merit ledger: the journal by month with category filter, search and Export; needs ledger read.
Audit log: search with action, resource and date filters; needs audit read (administrators and realm leads by default).
Dashboard: overview and to-do cells (approvals waiting on dispatch, the judgment queue); Karma statistics and Export Stats are for administrators.
