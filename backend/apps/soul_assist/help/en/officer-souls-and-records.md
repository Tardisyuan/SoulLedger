Souls (sidebar: "Soul Management"): "Create soul" needs the soul create permission; "Export", "Move to recycle bin" (the soul delete permission) and "Transfer" (the dispatch manage permission, one soul at a time) are in the batch bar. On a soul's page the actions follow its state: an alive soul can be marked dead and sent to judgment (the soul die permission), a soul under judgment gets "Start judgment" (the judgment create permission), a disposed soul can be reborn (the reincarnation reborn permission; refused while the original case's approval workflow is still running); "Edit" needs the soul update permission.
Importing souls from a file: on the Souls page, "Import" (the soul create permission) opens a dialog. Download the template, fill in one soul per row (name and civilization are required; birth and death dates, place of origin, birth name and description are optional), then choose the file. Every row is checked first and nothing is saved at this stage: errors are marked in the cell they belong to, and "Show only errors" narrows the table. The import button stays disabled while any row has an error, because the import is all or nothing. The civilization must be your own, a name with the same birth date as an existing soul counts as a duplicate, and a file has a maximum number of rows. After a successful import, "View this batch" opens the list narrowed to the souls just created.
Marking lost and recalling: when a soul goes missing on the way to its disposition and its state is Disposed, choose "Mark as lost" in the action menu at the top of the soul's page (the state transition permission). You must write a reason, which is required, length-capped, and goes into the soul ledger page and the audit log. While lost, the disposition deadline pauses; the page shows the "Lost since" date and who did it. Once found, tap "Recall" on the same page, again with a reason: the state returns to Disposed, the escort carries on under the same disposition, and the deadline is pushed back accordingly.
Fated date of death: "Edit" a soul and there is a "Fated date of death" field, only for Diyu souls, optional, needing the soul update permission; leave it empty if unknown and the soul is judged as usual. If the registered death is earlier than the fated date, the death is untimely: the judgment is marked "Untimely death", the soul waits in the City of Wrongful Death, and the judgment queue shows "Held until <date>"; it cannot be claimed before that day, and after it the hold no longer applies. The mark is stamped at the moment the death is registered, using the fated date then on file, and does not change afterwards; the edit form previews it when an already registered death is earlier than the fated date.
Filling in regions of origin: "Fill in regions of origin" on the Souls page (the soul update permission) updates the region of origin of existing souls from a list; only this hall's souls, and nothing is created. Three steps: choose the file, preview, update. The first column is the soul ID (the first column of an export), or the whole file may use soul codes instead, but only one kind per file; fill the region as the template says, and blank means unknown. The preview marks each row as will update, no change, not found, invalid value or repeated in the file; the last three are skipped without affecting the rest, and you can download the skipped rows, fix them and upload again. Greek halls use the region of origin to decide who presides; unknown goes straight to the final trial.
Mengpo's soup: when a Diyu soul is awaiting rebirth, its action area on the soul's page has a "Mengpo's soup" block showing Not taken or Taken with a time. It must be taken before rebirth; rebirth without it is refused and the soul stays awaiting rebirth, and a batch rebirth lists those who have not taken it. Tap "Record as taken" (the reincarnation manage permission) and confirm; it is once per life, cannot be undone, and clears when the rebirth completes. Souls of other civilizations do not need it.
Dedication of merit: a rite the soul's living relatives held for it. An officer enters it as a merit/demerit record with the category "Dedication of merit", filling in the source (who, which rite) and the date of the rite; it counts as merit only and only for souls being judged or serving a sentence. In the ledger such a record carries a small "⇠ From the living" tag.
Merit ledger: the journal by month with category filter, search and "Export"; needs the ledger read permission. When a role has a data scope, the entries list only souls within it, while the totals on the page stay the whole hall's, and the page says so.
Besides its entry and weight, a merit/demerit record can carry the article it is filed under (the article's text is frozen with the record when cited, so a later revision does not change it), how many occasions it covers, the stage of life (childhood / youth / adulthood / old age) and the evidence source (registry / witness / self-account / other, with an optional note); anything not recorded shows as unrecorded on the ledger and the judgment desk rather than a default. The weight is the row's total; it is not multiplied by the count. Adding or editing records needs the merit and demerit record manage permission; a record a concluded judgment admitted cannot be changed.
Audit log: search with action, resource and date filters; needs the audit read permission (administrators and realm leads by default). The resource filter has a "Hall settings" choice: the records of edits to a hall's own settings. An administrator who belongs to no hall also gets a "By hall" dropdown that narrows the log to one hall, and can read every hall; anyone bound to a hall, administrators included, sees only that hall's records, counts and timelines, and has no such dropdown. The same page's "Online sessions" tab lists the officers and souls signed in right now (the session manage permission, administrators by default) and can sign out one device or all of a person's devices; an administrator bound to a hall sees only that hall's people.
Dashboard: overview and to-do cells (approvals waiting on dispatch, the judgment queue, and for administrators a "Death sync anomalies" cell); "Deed Statistics" needs the ledger read permission, "Export Stats" the ledger export permission, and recalculating merit the merit calculation permission.
