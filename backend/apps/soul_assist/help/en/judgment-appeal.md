This is about appealing a judgment. It is a different thing from appealing a rejected rebirth application, which is in rebirth-appeal.
Where: on the "This life" page, every concluded judgment has a line at the bottom of its card. When you can appeal it says "Appeal by <date>" with "Appeal ›"; when you cannot, it says why. If you have appealed, there is one more line "Appeal no. N · status"; tap it to see each round.
Who can: only the original judgment of this life, once concluded. Judgments from past lives are read-only. Greek souls cannot appeal, because the judgment of Minos is final. A soul devoured by Ammit cannot appeal either, and an appeal still open at that moment is voided.
Deadline and count: after a judgment concludes there is an appeal period, and the last day is written on the card; for a second appeal the period is counted again from the day the first appeal was decided. Each judgment can be appealed at most twice, and a withdrawn appeal counts as one. Only one appeal without a result can be open at a time.
Filing: tap "Appeal", write your reason (required, with a length limit), add new evidence if you have any, and tap "Submit appeal". Leaving with unsubmitted text asks you first. A different officer reviews it, never the one who gave the original judgment. The assistant does not file it for you; you do it yourself on that page.
Statuses: awaiting review, under review, verdict stands, verdict changed, withdrawn, void. An appeal awaiting review or under review can be withdrawn, and the page tells you how many appeals would be left. After a result the card shows the reviewer's note; when the verdict is changed it shows "old verdict → new verdict".
What a changed verdict does: stations of the sentence plan not yet served follow the new verdict; each station already served earns you one "compensation" merit entry, already added to this life's entries and balance, marked with a small "↺ Verdict changed" tag.
Escalating: when a review has upheld the verdict, you still have an appeal left and the period has not closed, the same line shows "Escalate ›", using the same form. It goes through your civilization's appeal court station by station: a rejection at any station leaves the verdict standing, and when it clears all of them the last station decides. It cannot be withdrawn while the court is sitting, and it counts as one appeal.
