A soul of the Chinese underworld walks thirteen stations after death: the Earth God's Temple, the Road of the Yellow Springs, the Terrace for Viewing Home, the Village of Vicious Dogs, Golden Rooster Hill, the Village of Wild Ghosts, the Hall of Bewilderment, the Pavilion of Offerings, the Fort of the Ghost Realm, the City of Fengdu, the Eighteen Hells, the Lotus Terrace and the Cliff of Returning Souls.
On the "This life" page, the "To the underworld · thirteen stations" block shows which station you are at and what happens there. While you are alive you are not on the road, and the block is not shown.
How you move on: registering your death brings you to the Earth God's Temple; entering any of the Ten Courts brings you to the City of Fengdu; drinking Mengpo's soup brings you to the Cliff of Returning Souls. The other stations are recorded by the hall's officers, who also correct a wrong one; you cannot move yourself, and neither can the assistant. Once your rebirth is complete, the new life is not on the road and the block goes away.
Continent of birth: which of the four continents this life was born in (Pūrvavideha in the east, Jambudvīpa in the south, Aparagodānīya in the west, Uttarakuru in the north). It is shown on the "This life" page, or "Unknown" if not recorded. When you are reborn into the human path, the rebirth sets the next life's continent; unless the officer picks another, it stays the same as this life's. Rebirth into any other path has no continent.
Capture warrant: the City God issues it, the escorts capture you and hand you to the City God; when your death is registered, this warrant is issued and marked as handed to the City God at the same time. On the "This life" page the "Capture warrant" block says the escorts have handed you to the City God, with the date it was issued; once you are on the road it folds into the facts beside your birth and death as a single line. A revoked warrant is not shown, and other civilizations have no capture warrant.
