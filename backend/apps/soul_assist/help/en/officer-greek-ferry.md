All of this applies only to Greek halls. Charon ferries only the buried who can pay. When you edit the soul, "The crossing" is one of: buried, fare paid; unburied; no fare.
- An unburied soul, or one with no fare, waits on the bank of Acheron: its original judgment cannot be claimed, and the judgment queue shows no "Claim" but "Unburied: can't cross the river" or "No fare: can't cross the river". Record "Buried, fare paid" and the case can be claimed. Only the original judgment is held back; appeals and reopened cases are not.
- The unburied also cross once a hundred years have passed since death, and the soul page then says "A hundred years on the bank: may cross". A death date missing its month or day is counted from the latest date it could be; a soul with no recorded year of death stays held. A soul with no fare never crosses by waiting.
