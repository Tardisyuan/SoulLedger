---
Dying before your fated date (the date of death fixed in the ledger of life and death) is an untimely death: your judgment is marked Untimely, and you are held in the City of Wrongful Death and judged only from your fated date. On the "This life" page, the card of that judgment has a line at the bottom saying until when you are held, how far your grievance has got, or "Out of the City of Wrongful Death".
This is a grievance to the City God. It is not the same as appealing a judgment's outcome; for that, see judgment-appeal.
Filing: only while you are still held and the judgment is not concluded; the line then has "File a grievance ›" on the right. Write down your grievance (required, with a length limit) and tap "Submit grievance". An officer of the hall can also record it for you. A case can have only one grievance awaiting the City God at a time. The assistant does not file it for you; you do it yourself on that page.
Review: the City God's officers, or a hall administrator, look into it; the grievance level is recorded by the hall. A grievance that is major or severe and proves true releases you early, and your judgment can then be heard; a lesser grievance cannot be upheld. If it is not upheld you stay until your fated date, and may file again. Once reviewed, the City God's note is shown under the line.
Once your fated date has come, or you have been released early, you are no longer held and there is nothing to file.
