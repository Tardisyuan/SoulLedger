A Chinese underworld case whose recorded death came before the fated date is marked Untimely: the soul is held in the City of Wrongful Death and the case cannot be claimed before that date. The "City of Wrongful Death" block on the judgment desk, under the identity group in "Case materials", appears only on such cases.
- Cause of death (accident, criminal victim, suicide, war, other) and grievance level (none, minor, major, severe): editable with the execute judgment permission while the case is open; refused on a case that is not untimely, and fixed once concluded.
- Filing: while the soul is held and the case open, write the grievance (required) and click "File grievance"; this needs the execute judgment permission. The soul can also file from the soul app, under the same rules. A case with a grievance awaiting review or already upheld cannot take another; after one is not upheld, a new one can be filed.
- Review: needs the execute judgment permission, and you must be one of the officers of the case's city god, or a hall administrator with the reassign judgment permission; a case not sent to a city god can be reviewed only by the latter. A review note is required.
- "Upheld · release early…" is available only when the grievance level is major or severe, and greyed out otherwise. Upholding releases the soul at once: the case can be claimed and concluded straight away, and this cannot be undone. "Not upheld" keeps the soul held until the fated date.
- Once the fated date has come, the soul has been released, or the case is concluded, filing and review are refused. Filing and each review outcome are recorded on the soul's timeline.
