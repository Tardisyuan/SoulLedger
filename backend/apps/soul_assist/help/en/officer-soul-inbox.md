Hall inbox: letters souls write to this hall. Folders: All letters, Open, Closed, Awaiting reply, Replied, Drafts, Archived, Handed to me. Unread, archived and draft are per officer.
- Reading needs soul inbox read; Send, Save draft, Hand to a colleague…, Take back, and reply templates need soul inbox reply. A closed conversation cannot be replied to.
- Archiving is only for me; a new letter from the soul brings the conversation back out of the archive.
For how many letters are in each folder, use the inbox_counts tool; it names no souls.
Initial passwords awaiting delivery (soul credentials) lists soul accounts whose initial password, or a password issued by a reset, has not been delivered yet. Viewing needs soul account read; View password, Mark delivered, Retry sending and Go reset need soul account manage.
