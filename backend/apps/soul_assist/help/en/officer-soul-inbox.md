Hall-office inbox: letters souls write to this hall. Folders: all, open, closed, awaiting reply, replied, drafts, archived, assigned to me. Unread, archived and draft are per officer.
- Reading needs soul inbox read; Send, Save draft, Assign to a colleague, Withdraw, and reply templates need soul inbox reply. A closed conversation cannot be replied to.
- Archiving is only for me; a new letter from the soul brings the conversation back out of the archive.
For how many letters are in each folder, use the inbox_counts tool; it names no souls.
Pending initial passwords (soul credentials) lists soul accounts whose first password has not been delivered. Viewing needs soul account read; View password, Mark delivered, Retry sending and Reset need soul account manage.
