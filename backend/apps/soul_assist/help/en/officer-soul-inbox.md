Hall inbox: letters souls write to this hall. Folders: "All letters", "Open", "Closed", "Awaiting reply", "Replied", "Drafts", "Archived", "Handed to me". Unread, archived and draft are per officer.
- Reading needs the soul inbox read permission; "Send", "Save draft", "Hand to a colleague…", "Take back", and reply templates need the soul inbox reply permission. A closed conversation cannot be replied to.
- Archiving affects only you; a new letter from the soul brings the conversation back out of the archive.
For how many letters are in each folder, use the inbox_counts tool; it names no souls.
"Initial passwords awaiting delivery" lists soul accounts whose initial password, or a password issued by a reset, has not been delivered yet. Viewing needs the soul account read permission; "View password", "Mark delivered", "Retry sending" and "Go reset" need the soul account manage permission.
