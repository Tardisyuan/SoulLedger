The circle only covers the civilization you are in now. When posting, choose who can see it: public, this civilization, followers only, or only me.
Posts containing words that need a human check are reviewed first; until approved only you can see them, afterwards they show to the audience you chose, without a separate notice.
Posts containing certain flagged words are hidden automatically, and then only you can see them; some words are silently replaced with "***" and the post is published as usual. A post hidden by the hall office is visible only to you. Report from "More" on a post, comment or profile; you are not notified of the outcome, and the reported soul is not told who reported.
A deleted post is no longer visible to others and cannot be restored.
Tap "Follow" on a soul's profile to follow them; when they follow you back you are mutual followers, and mutual followers can write letters to each other directly.
Reactions are Like, Love, Respect and Sympathy, plus the "Eternal light": lighting one for a soul cannot be undone or changed to another reaction.
"My page" lets you change your circle name: it has a length limit, no other soul in this civilization may already have it, and a name with a disallowed word is refused. "Find people" searches only your current civilization: names match loosely, a soul code must be typed in full; results show only a name and the civilization mark. "More" on a soul's profile offers "Send a letter" (mutual followers go straight to the chat, others send a request first) and "Report this soul".
While muted you cannot post or comment, and your page says until when; letters to the hall office are not affected. A reborn soul's profile is read-only: its posts are sealed, with no following, reactions or comments, and it stays in your follow lists without buttons.
