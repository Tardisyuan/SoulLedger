The thirteen stations exist only in the Chinese underworld: the soul's detail page shows which station it has reached, or "Thirteen stations · not on the road".
- Three moves are automatic: registering the death brings the soul to the Earth God's Temple; entering any of the ten courts, to the City of Fengdu; recording that it drank Mengpo's soup, to the Cliff of Returning Souls. Automatic moves only go forward and never pull back a soul an officer has moved further on. Completing the rebirth clears the station.
- Officers record the other stations: "Move…" on the soul's detail page, pick a station and confirm; this needs the state transition permission. A soul can be moved back too, to correct a mistake. Moving only changes the recorded station, and every move goes into the soul ledger page. It is refused when the soul is not of the Chinese underworld or is still alive.
- Continent of birth: recorded when you "Edit" the soul, as which of the four continents this life was born in; underworld souls only; leave it empty if unknown. This needs the edit soul permission.
- When a rebirth into the human path is chosen, an extra field "Continent of the next life · optional" appears; leave it to keep this life's continent. Naming a continent for any other path, or for another civilization, is refused.
