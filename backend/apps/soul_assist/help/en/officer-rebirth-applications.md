Rebirth applications come from souls in the soul app. Statuses: under review, approved, rejected, appealing, appeal rejected, filtered on this page.
Review steps are assigned by role: a first application goes to a judge's first review and then to the final review by an administrator; an appeal goes to a judge's appeal review and then an administrator's final appeal review. Realm leads (MODERATOR) hold no step, but a realm lead or an administrator can escalate a rebirth or appeal workflow past its current step (workflow escalate, with a written reason), and escalating the last step approves the application, with no judge or administrator having decided.
The page lists applications and shows a detail dialog. In that dialog the cross-civilization choice (Cross-civilization / Within this civilization) is made, only while the first review is pending and only by that step's approver; approving or rejecting is done on the approval workflow page, reached with Open the approval workflow. Rejecting requires a reason written for the soul, which the soul will read.
Viewing needs workflow read; deciding needs workflow approve and being the step's approver.
