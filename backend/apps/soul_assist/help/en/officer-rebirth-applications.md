Rebirth applications come from souls in the soul app. Statuses: "Under review", "Approved", "Rejected", "Under appeal", "Appeal rejected"; the page can be filtered by them.
Review steps are assigned by role: a first application goes to a Judge's first review and then to the final review by an Administrator; an appeal goes to a Judge's appeal review and then an Administrator's final appeal review. Realm leads hold no step, but a Realm Lead or an Administrator can escalate a rebirth or appeal workflow past its current step (the workflow escalate permission, with a written reason), and escalating the last step approves the application, with no Judge or Administrator having decided.
The page lists applications and shows a detail dialog. In that dialog the cross-civilization choice ("Cross-civilization" / "Within this civilization") is made, only while the first review is pending and only by that step's approver; approving or rejecting is done on the approval workflow page, reached with "Open the approval workflow". Rejecting requires a reason written for the soul, which the soul will read.
Viewing needs the workflow read permission; deciding needs the workflow approve permission and being the step's approver (Judge or Administrator by default).
The page also has a "Cooldown shortening" tab: a soul in cooldown can ask for this cooldown to end earlier, once per cooldown. Approving sets how many days are still to wait from today (less than the days left; zero lets the soul apply again at once); rejecting needs a note written for the soul; the hall's cooldown setting is not changed. Viewing needs the workflow read permission, approving or rejecting the workflow approve permission; the result is pushed to the soul.
