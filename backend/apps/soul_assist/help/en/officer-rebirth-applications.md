Rebirth applications come from souls in the soul app. Statuses: under review, approved, rejected, appealing, appeal rejected, filtered on this page.
Review steps are assigned by role: a first application goes to a judge's first review and then to the final review by an administrator; an appeal goes to a judge's appeal review and then an administrator's final appeal review. Realm leads are not part of it.
The page lists applications and shows a detail dialog; the decision itself is made on the approval workflow page, reached with Open the approval workflow. Whether the soul is reborn into another civilization is decided only at the first review, by that step's approver. Rejecting requires a reason written for the soul, which the soul will read.
Viewing needs workflow read; deciding needs workflow approve and being the step's approver.
