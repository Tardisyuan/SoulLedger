  - "how do I restore something I deleted"
  - "can I delete permanently"

Recycle bin: deleted records. Listing needs the recycle bin read permission; an administrator who belongs to no hall sees every hall, everyone else (administrators bound to a hall included) only their own.
- "Restore" brings back everything that was deleted together with the item, not one row. It can be refused when something it depends on (for example a role a workflow template names) no longer exists.
- "Delete permanently" applies only to reference data (roles, menus, workflow templates, dispatch drafts, circle posts and comments), and only after the retention period. Souls are records and can never be deleted permanently.
Both "Restore" and "Delete permanently" are for administrators only; an administrator bound to a hall acts only on that hall's records.
