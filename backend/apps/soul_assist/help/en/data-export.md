Settings > My data > "Export my data" packs what the hall has recorded about you into one file.
Included: basic profile and your number, merit and demerit records, judgment results, letters and circle posts (with images), and recent sign-ins. Not included: officers' deliberation among themselves, and letters not addressed to you.
Requesting: tap "Enter your password and request" and enter your current password to show it is you (if face sign-in is on, confirm with your face). Only one can be prepared at a time. Usually a few minutes, up to a day if there is a lot; you can leave the page and you will be told when it is ready.
When ready, tap "Save or send…" to open the system share sheet and save it or send it to yourself. The file can be fetched only for a while; the page says "Available until" a time. After it expires, tap "Request again". If this attempt did not work you can also request again.
