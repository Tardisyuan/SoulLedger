Letters has two kinds of conversation:
- Hall office: write to the hall office of the hall you are in now; officers reply in the office's name, not instantly and with no fixed reply time; the reply appears in Letters, and you are notified if letter notifications are on. Use it for questions that need a person.
- Souls: souls who follow each other can talk freely; you can also find a soul by its full soul code. Before you follow each other you may send one letter, and until they reply or follow back you must wait before sending another; the app shows when you can.
While muted you cannot write to souls but can still write to the hall office. "Letters are not open here yet" means your hall has not enabled letters.
