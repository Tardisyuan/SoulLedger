A rejected application can be appealed, once per application. Open it on the Rebirth applications page, tap "Appeal", write the appeal statement and submit.
Only this life's applications that are "Rejected" and not yet appealed can be appealed; the tool's can_appeal is exactly that check.
Appeals are reviewed by the home civilization. A cooldown starts as soon as an application is rejected (whether or not you appeal; a rejected appeal starts it again), and no new application can be submitted during it; its end date comes from the tool.
The rejection reason is shown in the application's detail; after an appeal the first rejection reason and the appeal rejection reason are shown separately.
