A rejected application can be appealed, once per application. Open it on the "Rebirth applications" page, tap "Appeal", optionally write an appeal statement, and submit.
Only this life's applications that are "Rejected" and not yet appealed can be appealed.
Appeals are reviewed by the home civilization. A rejection starts a cooldown during which you cannot submit a new application. Appealing cancels that cooldown; if the appeal is rejected, a full new cooldown starts on the day of that rejection; if it is approved, there is none. The end date is shown on the page.
During a cooldown you can ask for it to be shortened: under the cooldown line on the "Rebirth applications" page, tap "Ask to shorten the cooldown", give a reason and submit (you may also fill in "Days you hope remain", how many days you hope are left after approval, zero meaning end at once; it must be less than the days left now, and it only guides the officer); an officer of the home civilization decides. Each cooldown can be asked once; after a rejection the same cooldown cannot be asked again, and after an approval the new end date is shown in the same place.
The rejection reason is shown in the application's detail; after an appeal the first rejection reason and the appeal rejection reason are shown separately.
