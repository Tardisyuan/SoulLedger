A rejected application can be appealed, once per application. Open it on the "Rebirth applications" page, tap "Appeal", optionally write an appeal statement, and submit.
Only this life's applications that are "Rejected" and not yet appealed can be appealed.
Appeals are reviewed by the home civilization. A rejection starts a cooldown during which you cannot submit a new application. While an appeal is under review, the earlier cooldown no longer applies; if the appeal is rejected, a full new cooldown starts from that rejection; if it is approved, there is none. The end date is shown on the page.
The rejection reason is shown in the application's detail; after an appeal the first rejection reason and the appeal rejection reason are shown separately.
