A rejected application can be appealed, once per application. Open it on the Rebirth applications page, tap "Appeal", optionally write an appeal statement, and submit.
Only this life's applications that are "Rejected" and not yet appealed can be appealed; the tool's can_appeal is exactly that check.
Appeals are reviewed by the home civilization. A cooldown starts when an application is rejected, and no new application can be submitted during it; its end date comes from the tool. If you appeal, that first cooldown no longer applies while the appeal is under review (a new application is blocked anyway, because one is open); a rejected appeal starts a cooldown, and an approved appeal has none.
The rejection reason is shown in the application's detail; after an appeal the first rejection reason and the appeal rejection reason are shown separately.
