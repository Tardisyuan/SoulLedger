Notifications are reached from the bell icon in the top bar, which carries the unread count; the page shows only your own. Filters: all, unread; "Mark as Read" and "Mark All as Read". Some notices carry an action: a forgotten-password help notice offers "Open users" (only to officers with the user management permission) and "Not them · ignore"; a reassignment request offers "Open the case".
"Profile" (from the top bar) edits basic information and changes the password; no permission is needed. "Email verification" in the basic information shows whether your email is verified; if not, press "Send verification email" and open the link in the mail. Changing the email means verifying again. Only a verified email receives the reset mail from "Forgot password".
The welcome page has a few bands: a greeting, "Souls in this court" statistics (with links to the judgment queue, the state filter and the roster), "Pick up where you left off" shortcuts chosen for your role, "Recent Activity" (your own latest entries in the audit log), and the version in the footer. On your very first sign-in a separate "First-time setup" screen comes first; once done or skipped you land on the welcome page, and "Redo first-time setup" under the shortcuts shows it again.
