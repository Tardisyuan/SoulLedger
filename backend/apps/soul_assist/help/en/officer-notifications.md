Notifications are reached from "Notifications" in the top bar (a ◔ symbol on narrow screens) and show only your own. Filters: all, unread; "Mark as Read" and "Mark All as Read". Some notices carry an action: a forgotten-password help notice offers "Open users" (only to officers with the user management permission) and "Not them · ignore"; a reassignment request offers "Open the case".
"Profile" (from the top bar) edits basic information and changes the password; no permission is needed. The welcome page shows a greeting, soul counts, quick actions and recent activity.
