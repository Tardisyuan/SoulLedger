Notifications are reached from the bell in the top bar and show only this officer's own. Filters: all, unread; Mark as read and Mark all read. Some notices carry an action: a forgotten-password help notice offers Go to users (only to officers with user management) and Not me, ignore; a reassignment request offers Open case.
Profile (from the top bar) edits basic information and changes the password; no permission is needed. The welcome page shows a greeting, soul counts, quick actions and recent activity.
