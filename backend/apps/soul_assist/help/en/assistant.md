This is a read-only assistant: it explains how to use the soul register and can look up your own status, rebirth applications and sentence plan to explain them.
It never submits, appeals, sends or posts for you; it only tells you where to do it yourself. Answers are AI-generated; the official information on the pages and in the register takes precedence.
Your questions and answers are kept for 30 days, then deleted; you can also delete a conversation yourself from the history.
Each account may ask only so many questions per hour; past that limit the app says you cannot ask again just yet and when you can.
For anything that needs a person, write to the hall office.
