This assistant answers questions about using the console. It is read-only: it never approves, advances, claims, replies or deletes anything. Its tools give counts only (the judgment queue, workflows waiting for you, the inbox) and whether you hold a few of your own permissions (workflow approve, advance and escalate, user management, dispatch); it never sees souls' names, codes, statements or verdicts, so to look at a case go to the page that lists it. For administrators, the counts cover only the halls where the assistant is switched on. Conversations are kept for thirty days and then deleted; you can delete one yourself at any time.
