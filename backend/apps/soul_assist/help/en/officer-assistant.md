This assistant answers questions about using the console. It is read-only: it never approves, advances, claims, replies or deletes anything. Its tools give counts only (the judgment queue, workflows waiting for you, the inbox) and your own permissions; it never sees souls' names, codes, statements or verdicts, so to look at a case go to the page that lists it. Administrators' counts cover every hall. Conversations are kept for a limited period and then deleted; you can delete one yourself at any time.
