Social: the console's social feed, with a composer (Post) and follows.
Circle moderation reviews what souls post in the soul app. Tabs: Reports, Pending content, Word list, Mutes, Handled. Needs social moderate (administrators and realm leads by default). Copying another hall's sensitive-word list is for administrators. Deleted posts and comments go to the recycle bin.
