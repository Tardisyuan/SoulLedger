"Feed": the console's own social feed, where you can "Post" and follow colleagues.
Circle moderation reviews what souls post in the soul app. Tabs: "Reports", "Pending content", "Word list", "Mutes", "Handled". Needs the social moderate permission (administrators and realm leads by default). Copying another hall's sensitive-word list is for administrators. Deleted posts and comments go to the recycle bin.
