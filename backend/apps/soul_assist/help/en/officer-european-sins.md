All of this applies only to European halls.
- Mortal or venial: when recording a demerit, the "Mortal / venial" field offers venial, mortal · unrepented and mortal · absolved; left empty it is unclassified. Concluding reads only the admitted evidence: an unrepented mortal sin allows only Failed (hell), and Passed or Pending is refused; if every admitted demerit is classified and all are venial or absolved mortal sins, Failed is refused and the verdict should be Pending (purgatory).
- That second check is skipped when any demerit is unclassified, or the soul is recorded as denying the soul's immortality or as neutral in life. A Retry is not checked. Appeal amendments follow the same rules.
- The vestibule of hell: "Neutral in life" when you edit the soul, for one who lived neither for good nor for evil and took no side. When condemned with no cited deed pointing to a deeper circle, the soul goes to the vestibule, outside the nine circles; a deed citing a circle sends it to that circle.
- The Earthly Paradise: when a purgatory term ends, the soul passes through the Earthly Paradise (drinking of Lethe and Eunoë) before rising to heaven, and its path keeps that stop. A hall without the Earthly Paradise realm yet sends the soul straight to heaven.
