The sign-in page takes the account your court issued: account and password. Your account decides the civilization; you enter it on sign-in, and the marked one is where this device last signed in. The page also shows "Today's statute", a different one each visit, quoted verbatim from the corpus.
- "Keep me signed in on this device": tick it and you need not sign in again for a while; it applies to this device only.
- "Forgot password": enter your username and press "Notify administrator". No reset link is sent; officers of your hall who hold the user management permission receive a help notice, confirm who you are, and reset your password in "User Management".
- A wrong account or password says how many tries remain; after too many failed attempts in a row the account locks, and you can try again after the time the page shows, or ask your court admin to unlock it.
- If the server cannot be reached, what you typed is kept; check your connection and retry.
First time here? The welcome page is linked from the sign-in page.
