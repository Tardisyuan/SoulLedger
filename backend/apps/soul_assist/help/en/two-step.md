Two-step verification: once on, signing in asks for the six-digit number from an authenticator app after your password. The number changes every little while. It is under Settings > Account security > two-step verification, marked On or Off.
Turning it on: enter your current password to show it is you; put an authenticator on your phone (search the app store for "authenticator", any free one will do); tap "Open in the authenticator app", or if that does not open, copy the key and choose "enter manually" in the authenticator; then type the six digits shown for the Soul Ledger entry and tap "Verify · Next". The last step is saving your recovery codes. Leaving midway does not turn it on.
Recovery codes: a set of ten, each works once, shown only this once. Use "Copy all" or "Save as file". You must tick "I have saved the recovery codes somewhere other than this phone" to finish, and there is no going back from that step. Once it is on, Settings shows how many codes are left; when they run low, tap "Regenerate". The old ones stop working at once, and the new set is again shown only once.
Signing in: after the password, type the six digits; it is sent as soon as all six are in. A wrong number says how many tries are left; too many wrong tries pauses it, and the page says when you can try again. Tick "Do not ask again on this phone" and this phone is not asked for a while.
Phone not with you: tap "Use a recovery code" and enter one you have not used. No phone and no recovery codes: tap "No phone and no recovery codes", then go to an officer of your hall. They check who you are and turn it off for you; after that your password is enough, and you can turn it on again. A contact email cannot be used to turn it off.
Turning it off: on the same page tap "Turn off two-step verification"; afterwards only the password is needed and the remaining recovery codes stop working. Turning it off asks for the six digits from the authenticator or an unused recovery code; the password alone cannot turn it off. Changing phone: turn it off on the old phone first, then turn it on again on the new one.
