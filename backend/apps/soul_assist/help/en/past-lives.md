Yes. The Past lives page lists each earlier life's file: merits and faults, judgments, dispositions, rebirth applications, and what and when you were reborn as.
Past lives are read-only: a sealed file cannot be applied or appealed from, and nothing can be done there. Lives that were never entered in the register are not shown.
