Yes. The Past lives page lists each earlier life's file: merits and faults, judgments, dispositions, rebirth applications, and what and when you were reborn as.
Past lives are read-only: a sealed file cannot be applied or appealed from, and nothing can be done there. Souls of the Egyptian and European civilizations have no past lives, so there is nothing on that page for them.
