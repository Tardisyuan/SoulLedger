Two features of the officer app.
My numbers: the card at the top of the "Me" tab; tap it for the detail. It has two groups. Judgments shows "Concluded this month" (with the start date underneath, so a zero early in the month is not read as an error), "Concluded in all" and "Claimed, not concluded", and tapping the last one opens your own group in the judgment queue; Appeal reviews shows "Decided" and "In review". It counts only your own work, across all halls. Until you have claimed and concluded your first case it says there is nothing to count yet; if loading fails you get one line and "Retry", never stale numbers.
Photographing evidence: in a case's attachments there are three entries, "Take a photo", "Choose from photos" and "Choose a PDF". You can pick several; each file shows its own upload progress and can be cancelled. Location and device details are removed from photos on upload. Only images and PDFs are accepted, with a limit on each file and on the case's total; going over is flagged, and the page's message has the exact figures.
When you can add: you must have claimed the case, otherwise it says to claim it first; a concluded case's attachments are read-only; offline the three entries are greyed with the reason. A file that failed stays where it was: if the network dropped you can tap "Retry", and if the server refused it outright (wrong type, too large) you can only remove it.
