The all-halls overview is for the global administrator: one row per hall with its to-dos, open judgments, this month's rebirth quota and appeals awaiting review, and every cell opens the matching page.
- "Enter" opens a read-only view of that hall: the banner shows the hall, "Read-only" and the time it is valid until, and every write is greyed out. Each view lasts a limited time and is not extended; entering and leaving are both audited. When it expires the page explains so; go back to the overview and enter again.
- Under Settings › Halls a new hall needs a civilization. A hall can be deactivated but not deleted: its data stays, it just takes no new death registrations, and it can be reactivated at any time.
