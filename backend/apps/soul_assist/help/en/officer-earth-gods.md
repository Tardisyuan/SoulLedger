Earth gods exist only in the Chinese underworld: the level below a county city god, recording the dead locally and reporting to the city god above.
- "Settings › Earth Gods": viewing needs the view judgment permission; creating, editing and deleting need the reassign judgment permission. Give a name (unique within the hall), the places covered (townships, for display and filtering only), the city god above and the officers. Only Chinese underworld halls can have earth gods. Deleting one keeps the deaths it recorded.
- The city god above must belong to this hall; a ranked one must be a county city god, an unranked one may be chosen too, and it may be left empty. Officers must be officers of this hall who can declare death.
- "Record a death as reported by an Earth God": needs the declare death permission, and you must be one of that earth god's officers or a hall administrator. It goes through the same handling as death sync: the soul enters judgment, arrives at the Earth God's Temple of the thirteen stations, and the case goes to the city god above for review; with no city god above, the city god is matched by place of origin. A failed attempt is also kept, as a failed registration.
